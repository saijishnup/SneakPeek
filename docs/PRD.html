2. Stack

LayerPickWhy
ShellElectron (latest stable)setContentProtection(true) maps to WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE. Node 24 is already installed, Rust is not. The real Notch uses Electron too.
UIPlain HTML, CSS, vanilla JSTwo views and one panel. A framework would be overhead.
Importpdfjs-dist, mammothSmallest reliable PDF and DOCX text extractors. TXT is native.
StoragelocalStorage + userData/bounds.jsonScript and settings in the renderer, window bounds in main. No DB.
Packageelectron-builder NSIS one-clickInstalls once, so it launches fast. The portable build unpacked about 125 MB on every start.
Testsnode:test + Playwright for ElectronUnit tests for the importer. A smoke test asserts the window flags.

Rejected: Tauri. It gives a smaller binary but needs a Rust toolchain, and a personal tool gains nothing from that.

5. Behavior

ThingSpec
WindowDefault 440×112, scaled 60–140% by the Size setting, top center of the primary display. Always on top at screen-saver level, so it sits above full-screen Meet. No taskbar icon, tray icon only. Position persists. It re-pins itself on top on blur and every 2 s, in case another topmost app steals the spot.
CaptureContent protection is on at all times and re-applied after every show, to dodge the Electron hide/show bug.
Read modeText scrolls up at the chosen speed. Mouse wheel nudges the position. Lines above the reading line stay bright and lines below are dimmed slightly.
Edit modeDouble-click opens it. The window grows and scrolling pauses. Esc or Done saves and shrinks the window back.
SpeedMeasured in wpm, from 30 to 300 in steps of 10. px/s = wpm ÷ 60 × 12 × (font size ÷ 16).
ImportTXT is read as-is. PDF text comes from pdf.js, with paragraphs joined. DOCX goes through mammoth's raw text. Scanned PDFs are unsupported and show a message.
